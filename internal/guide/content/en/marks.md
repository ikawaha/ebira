A mark makes a note stand out in the list. This note has the red mark.

- Colors: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, gray
- Symbols: ⭐ ❗ ❓ ✅ 📌 🔥 💡 🚧 ⏳ ❌ 📩 🕒 👀

## Setting and clearing

Open the menu with the flag button of the preview, a right click in the list, or `M`, and choose. Choosing the current mark again clears it. Marks do not change the update time of the note.

## Names of the marks

"Names of the marks…" in Server settings… of the gear menu gives the marks meanings, such as red for urgent. The names are synced with the notes.

## Searching by mark

Type `mark:red` or `mark:star` in the search field, or a name you gave, such as `mark:urgent`, to find the notes with the mark.
