---
title: History
tags: [guide, safety]
---
Each time the title, tags or body of a note change, the earlier version is kept. The clock button at the top right of the preview lists the versions.

- Choose a version to see how it differs from the note.
- "Restore this version" brings its content back. What the note was before is kept as a version too.

## How much is kept

- Up to 50 versions of a note. Versions older than 30 days go, except the newest 5.
- Changes made in another editor or with git become versions when ebira notices them.
- Versions are kept only on this computer, and not synced.

## The trash

Deleted notes stay in the Trash. "Empty trash…" in the menu of the Trash deletes them all for good.
