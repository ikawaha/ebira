## Separators

The colored bars of this notebook, such as "Getting started" and "Writing", are separators. Put them between notes to split a list into sections.

- Insert: "Insert a separator above…" in the right-click menu of a note, or "Insert a separator…" with a right click in the empty space under the list
- Change or delete: the "…" of the separator, or a right click (or double-click to change it)
- Move: drag it

A separator can have a heading, a date and a color. It is not a note, so it is neither searched nor tagged. Separators show only in the manual order.

## Order

Change the order with the menu above the list, or `s`.

| Order | |
|---|---|
| Manual order | The order you make (the default of a notebook) |
| Created | Newest first |
| Updated | Newest first |
| Title | By the letters |

In the manual order, drag notes, or press `J` / `K`, to reorder them. Each notebook keeps its order.
