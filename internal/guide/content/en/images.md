---
While editing, paste an image, or drop one onto the editor, to add it to the note. The body gets a reference like this at the cursor.

```
![description](<note ID>.assets/<name>.png)
```

- Images are kept in the folder `<note ID>.assets/` next to the note, and move, go to the trash and are deleted with it.
- PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP are taken, up to 10 MB each.
- Save a new note once before adding images.

## Checking images

"Check images" in the gear menu lists the images no note uses, and the references to images that do not exist. Choose some to delete.
