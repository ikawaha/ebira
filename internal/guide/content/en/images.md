title: Images and files
tags: [guide, writing]
---
While editing, paste an image or another file, or drop one onto the editor, to add it to the note. The body gets a reference like these at the cursor: an image shows in the body, and another file, such as a PDF, becomes a link.

```
![description](<note ID>.assets/<name>.png)
[report.pdf](<note ID>.assets/report.pdf)
```

- Images and files are kept in the folder `<note ID>.assets/` next to the note, and move, go to the trash and are deleted with it.
- The images shown in the body are PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP. Other files may be of any type. Each may be up to 50 MB.
- Clicking a link opens a PDF in a new tab, and downloads another file. In the desktop app, any file is saved in the Downloads folder (with a number, as in "name 2", when the name is taken).
- Save a new note once before adding images or files.

## Checking files

"Check files" in the gear menu lists the images and files no note uses, and the references to files that do not exist. Choose some to delete.

- It shows how large the unused files are in all, and how large the chosen ones are.
- Files added within the last hour, which may belong to a note being edited, and files only earlier versions of notes (the history) refer to, which putting such a version back needs, are not chosen at first. The history is dropped after about 30 days, and then such files are unused like the others.
