---
title: Finding in a note
tags: [guide, search]
---
The search field at the top finds notes. To find words in the open note, use the magnifier button of the preview, or `f`.

1. Press `f` and type the words
2. The matches in the body are marked
3. Enter (Shift+Enter) or `n` / `N` go to the next or previous one
4. Esc closes it

Case and full or half width do not matter. The field stays open when you go to another note.
