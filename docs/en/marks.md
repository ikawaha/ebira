A mark makes a note stand out in the list. This note has the red mark.

- Colors: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, gray
- Symbols: ⭐ ❗ ❓ ✅ 📌 🔥 💡 🚧 ⏳ ❌ 📩 🕒 👀

## Setting and clearing

Open the menu with the flag button of the preview, a right click in the list, or `M`, and choose. Choosing the current mark again clears it. A right click on one of the notes chosen together marks them all. Marks do not change the update time of the note.

## Pinning with 📌

A note with the 📌 mark stays at the top of the list of its notebook, in any sort (the order, the creation or update time, or the title), and so it does in an opened sub notebook. A new note goes below the pinned notes. All notes, a tag, a search, the trash and the archive do not keep them on top.

- Pinned notes can be reordered among themselves by dragging or with `J` / `K`.
- A note does not move across the line between the pinned notes and the others; take the 📌 off to move it out.
- Separators go only below the pinned notes.

## Names of the marks

"Names of the marks…" in Settings… of the gear menu gives the marks meanings, such as red for urgent. The names are synced with the notes.

## Searching by mark

Type `mark:red` or `mark:star` in the search field, or a name you gave, such as `mark:urgent`, to find the notes with the mark.
