A note can have any number of tags. The notes of this guide have `guide`, and a tag of their topic, such as `writing` or `search`.

## Viewing by tag

Switch the side column to tags at its top (or with `T`), and choose a tag to list its notes from all notebooks. Clicking a tag in the preview does the same.

A new note made while a tag is chosen starts with that tag.

## Searching by tag

Type `tag:guide` in the search field to search only the notes with the tag ([How to search](search.md)). Typing `tag:` suggests tags.

Case and full or half width do not matter.

The counts of the tag list leave out the notes in the trash and the archive.
